Covering Farnworth

Farnworth, Kearsley and Little Lever.

Covering BL4, with the same vehicles, drivers and fixed pricing as everywhere else we run.

Farnworth is the closest of our regular areas to base, which matters more than it sounds. It means short-notice work is often possible here when it would not be somewhere further out, and it means the vehicle is not already an hour into its day before it reaches you.

Airport and city runs

The A666 runs down towards Manchester, so both the airport and the city centre are straightforward. As everywhere, we set the pickup from the flight rather than the postcode, and we watch the inbound so a late landing does not leave a group stranded.

School runs and contract work

Term-time routes are a large part of what we do. We hold passenger transport contracts with Bolton and Bury Councils, every driver is DBS checked including cover drivers, and we keep the same named driver on a route wherever we can, because that consistency is the point of a contract run rather than a taxi.

Days out and nights out

Moses Gate Country Park, the Lakes, the Dales, arena nights in Manchester and away fixtures. One vehicle, one price, and nobody in the group has to stay sober to drive.

Before you book

Farnworth questions we get asked most.

Do you cover Kearsley and Little Lever?

Yes, along with Stoneclough, Prestolee, Darcy Lever and Moses Gate. BL4 is our nearest regular ground.

Can you take a booking at short notice?

Often, yes — being close by helps. It depends on what is already committed that day, so ring or message and we will tell you straight away.

Do you do regular school runs here?

Yes. We hold passenger transport contracts with Bolton and Bury Councils, and every driver is DBS checked, including drivers covering a route.

Is it worth a minibus for a short trip?

For a group, usually. Once you are past about six people the taxis home cost more than the vehicle, and everyone arrives and leaves together.

How many seats do you have?

Vehicles run from 4 to 18 seats, always with a driver. Tell us the luggage as well as the headcount.
