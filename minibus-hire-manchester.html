Running into Manchester

The city run we make most.

A Bolton operator, into Manchester and back, priced as one journey rather than two.

We are based in Bolton, which is in Greater Manchester but is not Manchester, and the distinction matters when you are booking. It means we are not sitting on a rank in Piccadilly waiting for a job. It also means we know the roads in from the north properly, we know which streets you can actually collect sixteen people from, and we price the return leg at the start rather than leaving you to sort it out at midnight.

Almost every Manchester booking we take is the same underlying problem: getting a group there together, and getting the same group home afterwards without splitting into four cars and a surge fare.

Gigs, arenas and theatre nights

Co-op Live, the AO Arena, the Apollo, the Albert Hall, the Bridgewater Hall and the Palace. Twenty thousand people leave a venue in about fifteen minutes, and the trams and the apps both know it. We agree a collection point before you go — a street a couple of minutes’ walk from the doors rather than the doors themselves — and the driver is there when you get to it.

Match days and the cricket

Old Trafford, the Etihad, and Emirates Old Trafford for a Test or a Blast night. The football is straightforward getting in and slow getting out, so we set a collection point and a time in advance rather than trying to find each other at full time. Away supporters travelling through Manchester are ordinary work for us too.

Nights out, birthdays and work parties

Deansgate, Spinningfields, the Northern Quarter, Ancoats. The reason a minibus wins on these is simple arithmetic: past about six people, the taxis home cost more than the vehicle, and nobody in the group has to stay on lemonade. Christmas party season books up early, so the further ahead you tell us, the better.

Corporate, conference and hotel shuttles

Manchester Central, the university campuses, the hotels around Piccadilly and Deansgate. This work is usually several timed movements rather than one trip — hotel to venue in the morning, venue to dinner, dinner back — and we price it as a whole so the finance team gets one figure.

Days out, shopping and the markets

The Trafford Centre, the museums and galleries, and the Christmas markets, where parking is the entire argument for booking a vehicle. Groups from care settings, community groups and school parties all travel with us on the same basis: a named driver, a fixed price, and a drop-off close to the door.

Before you book

Manchester questions we get asked most.

Are you based in Manchester?

No. We are based in Bolton, which is in Greater Manchester. Manchester city centre is one of our most regular runs rather than our doorstep, and the only practical difference is short notice: the further ahead you tell us, the more likely we can cover it.

Can you collect us after a gig or a match?

Yes, and it is most of why people book us for Manchester. We agree the collection point and the time before the day, usually a street a short walk from the venue rather than the venue doors, so nobody is standing in a crowd trying to describe where they are.

How long does Bolton to Manchester take?

It depends far more on the hour than the mileage. A Sunday morning and a Friday five o'clock are not the same journey, which is why we set the departure time from what you need to be there for, and not from a fixed rule.

Do you do Christmas parties and work nights out?

Yes, and it is the busiest thing we do in December. Book early: the last two weekends before Christmas go first, and we would rather turn you down in October than let you down on the night.

Do you go to Old Trafford and the Etihad?

Yes, for home games, away days and the cricket at Emirates Old Trafford. We drop as close as the day's road closures allow and collect from an agreed point afterwards.

What about Manchester Airport?

That is a different kind of job and it has its own page. Airport work is flight tracked and priced separately, so see our airport transfers page for how we handle it.

Can you do several pickups across Bolton on the way?

Usually yes, within reason. Two or three stops is ordinary. More than that starts to add real time to the journey, so tell us the addresses when you ask and we will build them into the timing.

How many seats do you have?

Vehicles run from 4 to 18 seats, always with a driver. Wheelchair accessible vehicles are available — say so when you ask for the price.
