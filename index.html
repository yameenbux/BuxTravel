How quickly will I get a quote?

Most enquiries are answered within 30 minutes, day or night. Send us the date, pick-up point, destination and how many of you there are, and we'll come back with a fixed price. Complicated multi-day or multi-vehicle jobs can take a little longer because we price them properly rather than guessing.

How is the price worked out?

We quote a fixed price for the whole job, not a meter rate. It accounts for the vehicle size, total mileage, driver hours, waiting time and any tolls or parking. Long-distance and overnight work includes the driver's legally required rest, which is why a 300-mile round trip isn't simply double a 150-mile one.

The price we quote is the price you pay. If you change the job — extra stops, a later finish, more passengers — we'll tell you what that changes before we do it.

How do I pay, and when?

Payment terms are confirmed with your quote. For regular business, school and corporate work we can set up an account with invoicing rather than paying trip by trip — ask when you enquire.

What if I need to cancel or change the booking?

Tell us as early as you can and we'll do what we can. Cancellation terms depend on how close to travel you are and whether we've turned other work away to hold the vehicle — they're set out in full in our booking terms and confirmed with your quote.

Changes to timings, pick-up points or passenger numbers are usually fine with reasonable notice.

Do you track flights on airport pick-ups?

Yes. Give us your flight number when you book and we'll check the arrival time on the day. If the flight is early or delayed, the driver's timing moves with it — you're not left waiting, and a delay outside your control doesn't change the agreed price.

We can also arrange a meet and greet in arrivals with a name board, which is worth it for larger groups or anyone who hasn't used the terminal before.

How much waiting time is included?

A reasonable allowance is built into every quote, and for airport pick-ups it runs from the actual landing time rather than the scheduled one. If you know in advance you'll need us to wait — a wedding, a match, a day out — tell us and we'll price the waiting in properly so there's no dispute at the end.

How much luggage can we bring?

It depends on the vehicle and how full it is — luggage space and passenger seats compete for the same room. A 16-seater carrying 16 people and 16 suitcases is a different job from one carrying 10 people and hand luggage.

Tell us what you're carrying when you enquire — cases, golf clubs, instruments, sports kit — and we'll put you in the right vehicle. It's the single most common cause of problems on the day and it's completely avoidable.

Can you take wheelchair users or passengers with mobility needs?

Tell us what's needed when you enquire and we'll be straight with you about what we can and can't accommodate, rather than promising and hoping. That includes step height, folding wheelchairs, assistance getting aboard, and where the vehicle can realistically pull up at each end.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Bux Travel is a licensed PSV and private hire operator, all drivers hold the appropriate licences, and vehicles are maintained on a scheduled inspection programme and fully insured for passenger carrying. Schools, colleges and corporate clients who need to see documentation before booking can request it — just ask.

Do you do regular contracts as well as one-off trips?

Yes, and it's a good part of what we do — school and college runs, staff shuttles and standing weekly bookings. On contract work we keep the same driver on the route wherever we can, which matters more than most people expect: they learn the route, the pick-up points and the passengers.

What size groups can you take?

Anything from 4 to 18 in a single vehicle. Over 18, we run more than one minibus in convoy and keep them together so your group arrives at the same time rather than trickling in.

How far will you travel?

Anywhere in the UK. We're Bolton based and minutes from the M61, so the North West is our day-to-day, but London, the Midlands, Scotland and Wales are routine work rather than exceptions — including multi-day trips where the vehicle and driver stay with your group throughout.
