How it works

One vehicle, one price, flight watched.

The two things that go wrong on an airport run are a split group and a moving price. Neither happens here.

Bolton to Manchester Airport is about 22 miles and runs in roughly 25 to 40 minutes depending on the time of day. We build the pickup time backwards from your flight, not forwards from your front door, and we allow for the M60 at the hour it will actually be.

Airports we run to

  • Manchester, all terminals, the run we do most
  • Liverpool John Lennon and Leeds Bradford
  • Birmingham, East Midlands and the London airports for early long-haul departures
  • Ferry ports and Eurostar terminals on the same basis

Coming home

We track the flight. If you land late, the driver waits, and the price you agreed does not move because of it. That is the whole reason to book a minibus rather than gamble on taxis at the rank with a group of twelve and a trolley each.

Luggage, and how many seats you really need

A sixteen seat minibus does not hold sixteen large cases as well as sixteen people. Tell us the bags when you ask for the price and we will put you in the right vehicle, or send a trailer or a second vehicle if that is genuinely what the group needs. Getting this wrong at five in the morning is not something you can fix on the driveway.

Before you book

The questions we get asked most.

How much is a minibus from Bolton to Manchester Airport?

It depends on the number of seats, the time of day and whether you want the return leg booked at the same time. We quote one fixed figure before you commit, usually within 30 minutes.

What happens if my flight is delayed?

We track the flight and the driver waits. The price you agreed does not change because the aircraft was late.

Can you pick up at 4am?

Yes. Early departures are routine work for us and are priced the same way as any other run.

Do you cover the return journey too?

Yes, and it is worth booking both legs together. It is usually better value and it means the driver already has your flight number.

How many people and how much luggage fits?

Vehicles run from 4 to 18 seats. Tell us the number of cases as well as the number of people, because large luggage changes which vehicle you need.

Do you have wheelchair accessible vehicles?

Yes. Tell us when you ask for the price and we will send an accessible vehicle, along with the luggage space the group still needs.
